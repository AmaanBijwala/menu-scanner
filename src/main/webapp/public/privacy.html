Privacy Policy

The Socio Advert, by Bijwala Solutions, Mumbai, India. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

The Socio Advert makes QR stickers that shops and restaurants (our "clients") put on their counters. When you scan one, you can open the shop's Google review, Instagram or other pages, join the shop's VIP Club, and collect rewards such as a stamp card or a scratch card. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Scan statistics. When a sticker is scanned we record the time, which button was tapped, and a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address and browser type. We use it only to count visits; it does not tell us who you are.
  • VIP Club and rewards details you type in: your name, age, gender, mobile number and, for a scratch card, the bill amount.
  • WhatsApp number check. To confirm your mobile number for rewards, you send a short message such as "VERIFY ABC123" to our WhatsApp Business number. From that message we use only your WhatsApp number and the code. We do not read any other chats.
  • Rewards history: the days you collected a stamp or played a scratch card, what you won, and when the shop gave you the reward.
  • A cookie on your phone that remembers your verified number for up to 180 days, so you do not have to verify every visit.

How we use it

  • To run the stamp card and scratch card fairly (for example, one stamp or one scratch per number per day).
  • To show the shop owner their own VIP Club list and rewards, so they can give you your reward and send you offers on WhatsApp.
  • To show the shop owner visit counts and which pages customers open.

Who can see it

  • The shop whose sticker you scanned can see the details you gave at that shop. Other shops cannot.
  • Bijwala Solutions, which runs the service. Our own staff view masks your mobile number.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) processes the verification message you send, under WhatsApp's privacy policy.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while the shop uses our service, or until you ask us to delete them. Number-check codes expire after 15 minutes.

Deleting your data

To have your details removed, send a WhatsApp message or email to us with your mobile number and the shop's name. We will delete your VIP Club details, rewards history and number checks for that number within 7 days and confirm when it is done.
