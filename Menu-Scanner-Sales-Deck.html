Ab Printed Menu
Ka Zamana Gaya

Har saal lakhs rupay waste hote hain sirf menu print karne mein. Aur fir bhi — price change karo, reprint karo. Ek item hatao, reprint karo. Naukar kho gaya, menu phata — reprint karo.

🖨️
Printing Ka Chakkar
Ek baar ka menu print karo — ₹5,000 se ₹20,000. Fir lamination, fir binding. Kuch hafte mein tear ho gaya ya price change hua — fir se poora kaam.
₹5,000–₹20,000 / Baar
