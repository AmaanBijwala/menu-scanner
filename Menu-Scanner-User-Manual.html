What is Menu Scanner?

Menu Scanner is a complete digital menu platform for restaurants. Share your menu via QR code, capture customer leads automatically, upload photos, and manage everything from one simple dashboard — no tech skills required.

📱

Digital Menu via QR

Customers scan your QR code and instantly see your full menu — no app download needed.

👥

Lead Capture

Collect customer names, phone numbers, and WhatsApp consent automatically when they view your menu.

🖼️

Photo Gallery

Showcase your restaurant ambience and food photos to attract more customers.

📣

Marketing Campaigns

Send WhatsApp and SMS promotions directly to your captured customers (PRO plan).

Section 04

Menu Manager

The Menu Manager is where you add, edit, and delete all your food and drink items. Go to Menu Manager from the sidebar.

➕ Adding a New Item

1

Click "Add Item"

The add item form will appear on screen.

2

Fill in the details

Enter the item name, category, price, discount (if any), and description. Mark it as Veg or Non-Veg.

3

Upload a photo

Select a photo from your phone/computer. Accepted: JPG, PNG. Max size: 5MB. The image will be automatically cropped to a square.

4

Set availability

Toggle availability ON to show the item, or OFF to hide it temporarily without deleting.

5

Save

Click Save. The item appears on your public menu immediately.

✏️ Editing an Item

  • 🖊️ Click the pencil (✎) icon next to the item
  • 📝 Update any field — name, price, photo, availability
  • 💾 Click Save to apply changes instantly

🗑️ Deleting an Item

  • ❌ Click the red ✕ button next to any item
  • ⚠️ A confirmation popup will appear — click OK to delete
  • 🔄 The item is removed from your public menu immediately
📌

BASIC Plan: You can add up to 50 menu items. To add more, upgrade to the PRO plan. PRO plan has no limit.

Section 05

Categories

Categories group your menu items (e.g., Starters, Main Course, Biryani, Beverages). You must create categories before adding items.

1

Go to Menu Manager

The category section is on the right side of the Menu Manager page.

2

Type a category name

E.g., "Starters", "Biryani", "Chinese", "Beverages", "Desserts"

3

Click "Add Category"

The category is saved and appears in the category list and in the item-add form.

4

To delete a category

Click the × button next to any category. Note: items in that category will lose their category assignment.

💡

Best Practice: Create all your categories first, then start adding menu items. This saves time as you can assign categories while adding items.

📤
Section 06

Bulk Upload

Bulk Upload lets you add many menu items at once using an Excel or CSV file — perfect when setting up for the first time or importing a full menu.

1

Download the template

In Menu Manager, click "Download Template". Open the Excel file on your computer.

2

Fill in your items

Each row = one menu item. Fill in Dish Name, Category, Price, Discount, Display Order, Description, Veg (Y/N), Available (Y/N).

3

Save the file

Save as .xlsx (Excel) or .csv format.

4

Upload the file

Click "Bulk Upload" in Menu Manager, select your file, and click Upload.

5

Check results

You'll see how many items were added successfully, and any rows with errors will be listed with the reason.

⚠️

Important: The Category column must exactly match a category you have already created. Make sure to create all categories before uploading. Spelling must match exactly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do customers need to download an app to view the menu? ▼
No. Customers simply scan your QR code with their phone's default camera app — no app download required. The menu opens directly in their browser.
How quickly do menu changes appear to customers? ▼
Changes appear within seconds. When you add, edit, or delete a menu item, the public menu is updated and the cache is refreshed automatically. Customers who refresh their browser will see the latest menu instantly.
What image formats and sizes are supported for food photos? ▼
JPG and PNG images are supported. Maximum file size is 5MB per image. Images are automatically cropped to a square and resized to 800×800 pixels for optimal display.
Can I hide an item temporarily without deleting it? ▼
Yes. When editing an item, simply toggle Availability to OFF. The item remains in your database but is hidden from the public menu. Toggle it back ON anytime to show it again.
How do I know when a new customer has filled the "Get Offers" form? ▼
Go to the Leads section in the sidebar. Your customer list updates in real time whenever someone fills the form. You can also export the list as a CSV file to view in Excel.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▼
Please contact your account manager or system administrator to reset your password. Once logged in, you can change it anytime from Profile Settings → Change Password.
Can I use Menu Scanner on my mobile phone? ▼
Yes! Menu Scanner is fully mobile-responsive. You can manage your menu, view leads, and update your profile from any smartphone browser. On mobile, navigation is available through the bottom navigation bar.
What is the difference between WhatsApp and "Get Offers"? ▼
The WhatsApp icon (from your social links) opens a direct chat with your restaurant's WhatsApp number. The "Get Offers" button is a lead capture form — customers enter their details and you save them to your customer database for future marketing.
How many categories can I create? ▼
There is no limit on categories — you can create as many as you need on both BASIC and PRO plans. Examples: Starters, Main Course, Biryani, Chinese, Beverages, Desserts, etc.
Is my customer data safe? ▼
Yes. All customer data is stored securely on our server. Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity for added security.
